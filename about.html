About us

Independent advice, senior attention

Littlebrook Consulting was founded on a simple idea: leadership teams get better advice from a small group of experienced people than from a large firm's junior bench.

Our story

Why we started Littlebrook

Littlebrook Consulting was founded after years spent inside larger consultancies, watching good strategy get diluted by layers of process and junior staffing. We set out to build something different: a small, senior team that stays close to the work from the first conversation to the final result.

Today we partner with founders, executives, and boards across retail, healthcare, and manufacturing, taking on a deliberately small number of engagements each year so every client gets the attention a strategic decision deserves.

Our mission

What we're here to do

We exist to help leadership teams make better decisions, faster — and to leave them more capable, not more dependent. Every engagement is built around a clear question, a rigorous process, and a result the client can act on immediately.

Rigour

We test our own thinking as hard as we test yours. Recommendations are grounded in evidence, not opinion.

Candour

We tell clients what they need to hear, delivered plainly and respectfully — even when it isn't the easy answer.

Proximity

No account teams or hidden staffing. The people who scope the engagement are the people who deliver it.
