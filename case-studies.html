Delivery of Internal Communication Platform

The challenge

A medium size UK business required a method of sharing relevant and up-to-date content to all users via a method which could be easily updated to all Head Office staff, and more importantly, approximately 1,000 field staff.

Our approach

Engaged with a recommended 3rd party service provider, who demonstrated a number of variations of service platforms to test and agree on the most suitable. The platform was built with test data and soak tested to ensure suitability and ease of use to all types of user.

The result

Weekly Communications published from the Head Office function to all users easily, timely and in a manageable method enabling full document control to be utilised. Additional benefit was the platform became the business Intranet and document repository.

Delivery of Fire Safety Risk Assessment on-line Platform

The challenge

A London-based business required a method of enable the management of Fires Safety Risk Assessments and their supporting evidential content, as current supplier provided documentation in a format that was not user friendly and cumbersome.

Our approach

Engaged with a number of suitably qualified 3rd party service providers, who demonstrated their on-line portals where supporting evidence could be uploaded and attached where required. Additionally, suitably qualified assessors were provided to the business to carry out the on-site assessments to generate the documentation through the portal.

The result

On-line platform enabled responsible persons within the business to update, manage and resolve identified issues in real-time easily and in a more effective manner.
